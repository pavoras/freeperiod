## Full description

Your cycle, your space.

FreePeriod is a period calendar and daily diary in one. Free and open source, with no ads, no subscription and no account.

• Log your period with a tap and see your cycle day at a glance – as a calendar or as a cycle ring.
• Calendar estimates for your next two periods, based on your own entries.
• Optional: days with a higher chance of pregnancy, shown as a plain calendar estimate.
• Daily diary: mood, flow, pain, symptoms, discharge, tags and notes. Switch on appetite, sex drive, a medication log ("log what you take") or a pain diary. Rename, reorder or hide anything and add your own categories and icons.
• History with averages, logged symptoms and charts over a week, a month, 3 months or a year.
• A PDF summary of your entries, for example for an appointment.
• Reminders for your period, a pill or anything personal.
• Life phases: during pregnancy, after birth and in menopause the estimates pause and your diary stays.
• Six accent colours, light and dark mode, English and German.

Private by design
FreePeriod has no internet permission and no analytics. Your entries stay on your phone. Optional app lock, encrypted backup files – also automatic, into a folder you choose – and CSV export: you decide where files go.

Honest limits
FreePeriod is not a medical device, not a method of contraception and not a tool to help you get pregnant. All dates are estimates from your own entries and can be wrong. For questions about contraception, pregnancy or your health, talk to a doctor or pharmacist.

Everything is free. If you like FreePeriod, you can support it with an optional one-time purchase; it unlocks nothing.

For ages 13 and up.
